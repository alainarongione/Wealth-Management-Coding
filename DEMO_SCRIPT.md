# Pitch & Demo Script (5-7 minutes)

**Persona:** Dr. Maya Chen, 31, internal medicine resident (final year) in Pennsylvania, married
(spouse earns $55k), $240k of student loans, nonprofit hospital, no disability insurance.
Load her from Tab 1 → Saved clients → `Dr_Maya_Chen` → **Load**.

**Before class:** run the app once, click through every tab, and take a screenshot of each one
(or screen-record the walkthrough) as a backup in case the live demo fails.

| Time | Who | Tab | What to show and say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:45 | | Title | **Problem.** Doctors start earning at ~32 with $215k of median debt, then their pay jumps 5x. Generic apps say a resident is in the bottom 1% of her age group. That's true, and also useless. |
| 0:45-1:45 | | 1 · Profile | Net worth −$233k (1st percentile of under-35 households), but human capital is **$3.6M**. Show the lifetime balance-sheet chart. Point out risk tolerance 60 vs. capacity 62 and the capacity breakdown (no disability insurance costs 14 points). Show the longevity chart: plan to 91, not 82. |
| 1:45-2:45 | | 2 · Portfolio | The waterfall explains the 75% stock mix: age rule, tolerance, human capital, capped by capacity. Show the glide path. Show the **honesty check**: the "optimized" mix loses out of sample, so we use a rule. Fan chart: 76% chance of the goal. |
| 2:45-3:45 | | 3 · Life Events | Turn on a fellowship, house and two kids. Add the **2008 crash at 59**. The timing chart shows sequence-of-returns risk. Turn on disability without insurance and watch the odds collapse. Toggle insurance back on. |
| 3:45-4:30 | | 4 · Cash Flow | Switch to "First attending year." Show the Sankey. Loan table: RAP-then-payoff vs. PSLF. Goals dashboard: emergency fund and income protection are red; the savings plan is green, so she can afford to save more. |
| 4:30-5:15 | | 5 · Where to Practice | Map: same paycheck, ~$90k/yr more buying power in South Dakota than California. Physician mortgage vs. conventional break-even. |
| 5:15-5:45 | | Client Report | Download the one-page plan with the next steps. |
| 5:45-6:30 | | | **Under the hood:** CRSP + Fama-French (WRDS), FRED, SCF 2025, Medscape, AAMC, SSA, Tax Foundation, BEA, all cached. **Next:** account aggregation, specialty disability/malpractice risk, metro-level physician pay. |

**Backup lines:** if the live demo fails, switch to screenshots. If asked "why not optimize?",
show the out-of-sample table.
